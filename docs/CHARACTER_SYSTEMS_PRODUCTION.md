# Equipment, Skills, NPC and Dialogue Production

STATUS: ARCHITECTURE FROZEN FOR RECONCILIATION.

## 1. Equipment and inventory

Equipment remains physical, visible and character/body compatible rather than one universal slot template.

Routine durability must not become maintenance busywork. Meaningful breakage/damage comes from actual use, events, attacks, environments or mechanics.

Carried physical inventory obeys actual storage capability:
- body;
- bags/containers;
- vehicles;
- storage facilities;
- magical storage;
- Factors;
- pocket dimensions;
- other valid capabilities.

Ordinary small items may be aggregated for usability, but the game does not assume an arbitrary infinite 999-slot backpack.

Real storage abilities legitimately expand/bypass storage limits according to their rules.

### Item quality vs Rank

Equipment **Quality/Rarity** is separate from character Origin Rarity and does not reuse R/SR/SSR/UR/LR/MR by default.

Item-quality presentation may use world/culture-specific terminology while the engine stores normalized quality/potential data.

Item Rank is separate from Quality:
- Quality = inherent construction/material/potential identity;
- Rank = actual developed power/existence stage.

Equipment fusion/absorption exists only when an item, forge, ability or process supports it; there is no universal "feed five swords into one sword" mechanic.

Items may become semi-sentient/sentient through actual development/history/mechanics.

## 2. Crafting

Preserve both learned recipes and genuine experimentation/invention.

Crafting output may depend on:
- crafter;
- knowledge;
- materials;
- facility;
- environment;
- techniques;
- special conditions.

Commissioned crafting may specify:
- materials;
- intended function;
- appearance;
- desired properties;
- tolerances/priorities.

The crafting system remains slightly more simulation-heavy than a normal gacha RPG, but it does not become a machining/worker micromanagement simulator.

## 3. Skill development

Skills do not use one mandatory universal 1-100 progression model.

A skill may improve through:
- repetition/proficiency;
- comprehension;
- qualitative stages;
- breakthrough;
- transformation;
- experience;
- specific conditions;
- other authored mechanisms.

Repetition matters only where repetition causally develops the skill.

True forgetting/loss requires a real mechanic such as memory destruction, soul damage, sealing, anatomical incompatibility or equivalent.

Minor compatible discoveries may integrate naturally into the resolved kit. Major kit-defining fusion/evolution choices remain player-directed.

A skill may evolve into or contribute to:
- a Class-defining discipline;
- Factor interaction;
- item ability;
- Domain technique;
- World Manifestation component;
- another higher-order system.

Protagonist, characters and NPCs may invent genuinely original skills when their capabilities/history support it rather than only selecting from one global predefined catalog.

Provenance is retained.

## 4. NPC generation and promotion

Ordinary generated NPCs can become fully playable world companions when history makes them important.

World-born promoted NPCs remain unique world individuals and do not automatically become multi-Manifestation gacha Identities.

They may theoretically reach any systemic achievement if they genuinely qualify:
- high Existence Rank;
- Ruler;
- Overlord;
- Crown;
- Grand Class appointment;
- other normal systemic states.

Promotion from background cohort to persistent named entity occurs automatically when causal importance warrants it, not only through a manual player button.

Promoted NPCs preserve identity/history while being eligible for later bespoke upgrades:
- higher-quality model/portrait;
- voice;
- animations;
- kit depth;
- story content.

NPC simulation uses compact personality/goals by default. Complexity increases with importance rather than every villager running a full life simulator.

An originally insignificant generated NPC may organically become a major antagonist, ally, lover, ruler, Grand candidate or other major figure.

## 5. Dialogue architecture

Dialogue is hybrid:

- authored choices control high-stakes/irreversible narrative branches;
- contextual/free-text conversation may operate alongside them.

The offline core never depends on a cloud language model.

Free-text capability may use an optional/packaged on-device model or structured semantic parser over authoritative NPC/world state. When unavailable or disabled, deterministic authored/structured dialogue remains fully functional.

Free-text output is constrained by known facts, personality/state and content safety/validity; it cannot rewrite authoritative state merely by saying something happened.

Player-entered/selected protagonist dialogue is normally unvoiced. Key authored lines and combat/ambient barks may be voiced.

## 6. Knowledge, belief and memory

Named NPCs store meaningful semantic facts/events rather than unlimited verbatim transcripts.

NPC beliefs may be wrong.

The architecture supports:
- rumor;
- propaganda;
- lies;
- misidentification;
- outdated maps;
- false history;
- uncertain intelligence.

Rumors propagate causally through witnesses, travel, communication, institutions, media, magic/technology and other valid channels.

Dialogue options can depend on what the protagonist actually knows/senses/understands.

## 7. Language and writing

Real language barriers may exist between civilizations/species/worlds.

Languages can be learned normally or bypassed through valid translation capabilities:
- magic;
- implants;
- Factors;
- telepathy;
- artifacts;
- interpreters;
- other mechanisms.

Unknown writing/scripts similarly require actual knowledge/translation instead of automatic omniscient UI translation.

Language difficulty is used where it enriches the world rather than as universal repetitive friction.


## 8. Skins / appearance variants

Characters may own cosmetic **Skins** in addition to lore-significant Versions/forms.

A Skin:
- changes presentation/outfit/appearance without silently creating a new Character Identity or gameplay Version;
- may be equipped per Manifestation unless content declares a broader shared rule;
- can coexist with equipment visibility, clothing damage and mature-content presentation where the asset set supports it;
- is recorded in historical/replay presentation when relevant;
- may include alternate voice/UI/cut-in presentation only when explicitly authored;
- does not change combat stats unless it is actually a gameplay-bearing Version/form rather than a cosmetic Skin.

Archive/replay can use historical appearance or current appearance, including compatible Skin selection.

## 9. Equipment affinity and proficiency presentation

Equipment affinity behavior remains item-specific.

Default UI presentation:
- affinity is shown primarily through **named grades/milestones** and progress where useful;
- precise hidden/internal values may exist for deterministic state but are not shown unless a mechanic genuinely benefits from exact numbers;
- proficiency is shown separately from affinity and may use grades as the normal presentation;
- exact numerical proficiency values appear only where mechanically useful.

There is no universal affinity-to-stat formula.
