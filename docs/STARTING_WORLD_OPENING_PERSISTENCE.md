# Starting World, Opening, Recruitment and Save Continuity

This document records the current authoritative decisions for the starting world/opening, first recruitment path, narrative-spoiler policy, and single-world save continuity.

## 1. Starting world and opening

- The starting region/world is seed-generated. Once created, generated geography is canonical for the one persistent world.
- Tone: beautiful with a mild but credible sense of danger.
- The first world/dimension is broadly historical-medieval. Stronger/later worlds may trend into increasingly extreme high-fantasy, post-apocalyptic, cyber/technological and other genres.
- The protagonist awakens alone in plain wilderness. No mandatory dramatic ruin, battlefield or cosmic object is placed at spawn; ordinary local monsters/wildlife may be present.
- A short world-establishing cinematic is allowed, then control is given to the player.
- There is no conventional forced tutorial.
- The protagonist has no autobiographical memory of his past/origin. Functional knowledge required to act/communicate remains unless later story content establishes otherwise. Whether he came from elsewhere is initially unknown.
- Early play permits exploration before combat.

## 2. Early danger and first region

- The protagonist begins unarmed, so the first meaningful hostile encounter should communicate vulnerability and be avoidable rather than function as a combat tutorial.
- The starting dimension does not showcase late-game entities merely for spectacle.
- Preferred first serious elite/boss: optional territorial monster/creature grounded in local ecology, not a mandatory tutorial boss.
- Nothing is forced solely to teach a system.
- Because the biome is randomized, the main orientation landmark is seed/context-dependent and should fit the generated natural or historical-medieval environment.
- The initial region reads as one coherent biome rather than a patchwork of artificial sub-biomes.
- A settlement is discoverable after some exploration. Time to first meaningful intelligent-NPC contact depends on player exploration.

## 3. Factions, dungeon and territory

- Three relevant factions exist around the starting area.
- Their relations are state-driven rather than a fixed mandatory opening war. A baseline seed may include one active tension/conflict and a differently aligned third faction.
- At least one early faction may contain a low-level Ruler with ordinary Territory but no Domain.
- Ancient advanced-civilization ruins and dimensional anomalies are not foregrounded at the beginning.
- The first dungeon should fit the generated region/historical-medieval layer: cave, mine, crypt, fort/ruin or a hybrid. It is not a tutorial dungeon and may be discoverable long before it can be cleared.
- First Territory can be gained through any valid world route: unclaimed/abandoned development, conquest, transfer, etc.
- There is no scheduled first-Territory milestone. The player may remain territoryless for a very long time, potentially much of the game.
- First Domain-Core awakening is a later major progression event, not a tutorial step.
- Main Territory can eventually be anywhere; the spawn region is not privileged as the inevitable capital.

## 4. Production philosophy

- There is no small content-count target that defines a vertical slice.
- Once the core game is technically certified playable, production shifts heavily toward designing a large content pool before full play begins.
- Early boss/enemy/settlement counts therefore remain content-production decisions, not hard creative limits.

## 5. Ruler access and gacha

- The contract/gacha system becomes available only after the protagonist satisfies the actual requirements to become a Ruler.
- No tutorial-only access exception exists.
- The first pull is not a fixed scripted character and does not use a restricted introductory pool.
- Once Ruler/gacha conditions are met, normal banners are available according to the real world/system rules without tutorial gating.
- The initial pull resource is a small first-Ruler dimensional allocation/initial contract resource generated by the canonical preparation mechanism, not an out-of-world tutorial gift.
- First acquisition of a Character Identity may use a substantial summon presentation; duplicate-equivalent/repeat acquisitions may shorten it.
- A newly acquired Manifestation may itself be confused about the metaphysical acquisition situation when appropriate.
- No two opening companions are pre-granted before normal gacha.
- All pullable/gacha Character Identities are intended to be female. This does not constrain the sex of ordinary NPCs, enemies, faction members or Rulers.

## 6. Narrative authorship / spoiler rule

- The engineering/narrative assistant owns construction of the actual story rather than pre-exposing its plot outline to the Creative Director.
- Narrative should use the established preference calibration: FGO, Honkai Impact 3rd Part 1, NieR:Automata and Umineko qualities; long mysteries; recontextualization; metaphysical layers; dense meaningful information; tragedy; morally unclean conflicts; coherent antagonists; protagonist/Ruler POV; authored arcs subordinate to persistent history.
- Preference/world-rule questions may be asked extensively.
- Future plot solutions, twists, hidden identities and story answers should normally be kept secret so the Creative Director can experience them as a player.
- The exact point at which the protagonist learns the metaphysical truth behind the gacha/contract UI is therefore a narrative decision rather than a spoiler to predefine publicly.

## 7. Single persistent world and uninstall-safe recovery

- Player-facing model: one canonical persistent world/history, not routine parallel save slots.
- Starting over is an explicit destructive Clear Data / new-world action and requires strong confirmation.
- Live mutable state remains in the versioned SQLite-centered architecture.
- Uninstall protection requires backups outside app-private Android storage.
- Preferred implementation: rotating compact state snapshots in a user-visible persistent Documents/Downloads-style location selected/authorized through Android Storage Access Framework, plus manual Export Save.
- Save backups contain authoritative state, schema/version metadata, hashes and small required metadata, not duplicate installed content assets.
- Backups are integrity-checked, versioned and migratable; keep several recent snapshots plus less-frequent older snapshots rather than one overwrite-only file.
- Reinstall exposes Recover Existing World / Import Backup so the persistent history can be restored.
- Optional secondary archive/cloud copies such as TeraBox may exist later, but core save survival must not depend on cloud connectivity.
