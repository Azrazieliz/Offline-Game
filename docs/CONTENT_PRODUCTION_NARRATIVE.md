# Content Production and Narrative Continuity

STATUS: ARCHITECTURE FROZEN; CONTENT PRODUCTION IS ONGOING.

## 1. Character production target

Production aims for **both quantity and depth**.

Target cadence after pipelines are mature: approximately **2-3 fully polished characters per week**, without a hard lifetime roster cap.

"Polished" means the character package is production-complete for its intended tier rather than a placeholder body with a kit:
- distinctive design;
- combat kit;
- progression routes;
- character-specific animation expression;
- voice tier;
- equipment/Version integration;
- biography/state integration;
- World Fantasm content where applicable;
- meaningful mature-content support when eligible.

Shared rig/locomotion/technical foundations are allowed for throughput, but they may not make playable characters feel like reskins.

## 2. Lower-importance content

Lower-Origin-Rarity or initially lower-importance characters may use more shared technical foundations while remaining mechanically/personality/design distinct and fully functional.

They remain eligible for later bespoke upgrades/reworks/evolution rather than being permanently cheap content.

## 3. World production

Each major World/Dimension should launch as a coherent ecosystem:
- civilizations/factions;
- creatures;
- materials/resources;
- equipment;
- dungeons/locations;
- bosses;
- local progression;
- environment/traversal grammar;
- culture/development identity.

Production deepens the starting World into a long-term RPG space while parallel production seeds several later Worlds/Dimensions so dimensional progression has breadth.

## 4. World Fantasms and Domains

MR World Fantasms are character-specific mechanical realities, not generic rarity fields.

Domain abilities emerge from:
- Core Concepts/Aspects;
- Core history;
- fusion lineage;
- Territory;
- Ruler relationship;
- development.

There is no universal Domain skill tree that erases those identities.

## 5. Bosses and challenge content

Major bosses are primarily authored entities whose mechanics arise from what they actually are.

Procedural/variant bosses are allowed where appropriate, but generic affixes do not replace identity-driven design.

Lore-grounded repeatable/endless challenge content is supported:
- towers;
- survival;
- escalating realms;
- boss sequences;
- other appropriate structures.

Adaptive difficulty is local to the authored challenge mechanism and respects the reality's actual order/ceiling.

## 6. Narrative continuity

Major arcs may fail or resolve differently due to persistent-world causality.

If a planned story character legitimately dies before a later authored scene:
- the death remains canonical;
- the scene adapts, reassigns or disappears;
- a valid resurrection mechanic may restore them if the story/world actually supports it;
- heroification/Identity-record gacha access may preserve collectible access as defined in docs/GACHA_ECONOMY.md.

The protagonist may become cosmologically central enough that future arcs arise from the consequences of his own accumulated history.

There is no final credits state that permanently ends the persistent world. Major "final" arcs may conclude while higher/later history continues.

Cosmological revelations may recontextualize earlier understanding provided previously observed facts remain coherent rather than being arbitrarily retconned.

Actual plot solutions/twists remain spoiler-protected from the Creative Director unless explicitly requested.

## 7. Mobile feasibility doctrine

No accepted player-facing system is simply cut because it is expensive.

If profiling shows a system is too costly on the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra, implementation may be redesigned, abstracted, streamed, tiered or moved between local/remote-fidelity representations **provided the player-facing behavior and design intent remain materially the same**.

Performance engineering serves the design rather than silently deleting it.
