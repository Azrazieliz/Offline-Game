# Gacha Economy and Copy Architecture

STATUS: ARCHITECTURE FROZEN; NUMERICAL TUNING REMAINS.

## 1. Acquisition remains meaningful

Gacha is entirely gameplay-earned/offline. There are no real-money purchases or IAP.

Pull currency remains renewable indefinitely through valid game/world systems so the persistent world cannot permanently exhaust gacha access.

Acquisition should not be trivial: obtaining a desired character must still produce genuine pull excitement. However, acquisition is easier than fully maximizing/developing that character.

Long-term scarcity may concentrate in difficult development, evolution, Transcendence, rare conditions/materials and Grand Convergence rather than making basic acquisition intolerably drought-prone.

## 2. Existing pity foundation

The existing provisional foundation remains for later simulation/tuning:
- standard limited hard pity around 70 pulls;
- separate pity counter and featured guarantee;
- first qualifying top result may miss featured;
- after a miss, next qualifying top result guaranteed featured;
- compatible category carryover;
- banner-specific soft pity;
- multi-feature/designation support;
- ten-pull floor support.

Exact rates/thresholds are not frozen until deterministic simulation.

## 3. Repeat acquisition

Every qualifying repeat acquisition normally creates a **new full persistent Manifestation** of the Character Identity.

There is no hard copy cap.

Copies exist specifically so the player can develop divergent:
- Evolution;
- Awakening;
- Corruption;
- future route families;
- equipment/skill builds;
- Rank/Rarity states;
- other histories.

Copies are never automatically collapsed into fragments/material.

There is no generic "burn unwanted copy into shards" action in the frozen architecture. A copy can cease independent existence only through a real supported mechanism such as:
- Grand Convergence;
- character-specific fusion/absorption;
- another explicit metaphysical/systemic process.

Archiving/roster filtering may hide a copy from ordinary management without destroying it.

## 4. Grand Convergence visibility

The Identity page can show known Convergence-relevant completed/missing lineages.

Secret route requirements remain hidden until the player gains enough knowledge to discover them.

Grand Convergence remains an actual character-development finalization, not duplicate currency conversion.

## 5. Banner/world relationship

Permanent, limited, event/themed, faction/world and other banner families remain valid.

The World Director may select/schedule banners according to world/story relevance, sealed content, variety and valid timing without pretending the featured Identity is physically local.

## 6. Heroification / posthumous gacha preservation

If an important authored/potential story character dies in the persistent world before the player ever has a meaningful opportunity to encounter/recruit them, that history does not necessarily remove them from collectible content.

The dimensional preparation system may record/heroify a sufficiently significant dead Identity into a higher-order **Heroic Record / Identity Pattern**, analogous in function to a throne-of-heroes concept.

That record can later become contract/gacha accessible while preserving the fact that the original world individual died.

This is not a save rewind or resurrection of local history:
- the death remains canonical;
- the manifested gacha instance is a Ruler-specific Manifestation derived from the recorded Identity Pattern;
- story content adapts to the original death.

Eligibility for heroification is content/systemic and not guaranteed for every anonymous person.
