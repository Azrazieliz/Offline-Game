# Cosmology and Dimensional Structure

This document records the current authoritative large-scale cosmology architecture. It preserves the previously established graph of universes, dimensions, worlds and sub-realms, while making the higher metaphysical structure intentionally extensible for a virtually unbounded game.

## 1. Layered but graph-like reality

Reality is not one simple nesting tree.

The engine recognizes at least these scales:

- **Sub-realm** — a bounded local reality, pocket, layer, dungeon-realm, afterlife zone, sealed space, inner world or other subordinate space.
- **World** — a self-consistent major reality with its own environmental/civilizational state, laws and World Rank.
- **Dimension** — a higher-order metaphysical envelope/network that can contain, connect or sustain multiple worlds and sub-realms sharing deeper structural laws.
- **Universe** — a still broader continuity that may contain or intersect multiple dimensions.
- **Interstice** — the higher medium/network through which otherwise separate universes/dimensions can be related, separated, reached or isolated.
- **Outer Beyond** — a deliberately open category for reality that does not belong to the ordinary Interstice/dimensional structure.

These layers are graph nodes/regions with stable IDs and links rather than assumptions that every object belongs to one rigid parent forever. Cross-links, embedded realms, overlapping structures, folded spaces, dimensional scars and impossible-looking topology are therefore supported.

The cosmology is intentionally open-ended. Future content may introduce higher-order structures above or adjacent to the Interstice without invalidating prior worlds. No universal "final box containing everything" is required.

## 2. Dimensional metaphysical consciousness

Dimensions are **semi-conscious only in a metaphysical sense**.

They do not normally think, converse, form personalities or make human-like plans.

Instead a Dimension can possess a distributed homeostatic "will" expressed through:

- preservation of structural integrity;
- reactions to existential threats;
- compatibility/resistance toward foreign laws;
- stabilization of worlds/sub-realms;
- pressure against entities far above local tolerance;
- preparation/contract/gacha mechanisms;
- probabilistic bias or opportunity creation within allowed rules;
- emergency responses to dimensional damage.

This is closer to a metaphysical immune/homeostasis system than a person.

Rare avatars, interfaces, prophets, Core-like intermediaries or emergent intelligences may interpret a Dimension's impulses, but those intermediaries are not proof that the underlying Dimension itself is a conventional conscious character.

## 3. World Rank

World Rank is distinct from personal Existence/Power Rank.

It represents a world's current **stable reality capacity**, including:

- law complexity and integrity;
- energy/resource ceiling;
- dimensional anchoring;
- ability to sustain high-Rank beings and phenomena;
- resilience to Authority/World Fantasm/Domain-scale alteration;
- local metaphysical density/stability;
- connected higher structures and anchors.

World Rank does not directly say who would win a battle. It says what scale of existence and reality manipulation the world can naturally host without severe distortion.

## 4. World Rank evolution

World Rank can rise.

Upward evolution is slow and consequential rather than ordinary XP.

Possible contributors include:

- long-term accumulation/stabilization of high-grade energy;
- mature Domains and Domain Cores acting as reality anchors;
- repeated stable presence of high-Rank existences;
- world-scale cultivation/technology/magic that changes the underlying laws;
- dimensional fusion/merger;
- creation or repair of major metaphysical anchors;
- absorption of compatible sub-realms/world fragments;
- major historical transformations;
- deliberate world-evolution projects or artifacts;
- intervention by higher-dimensional mechanisms.

A single powerful Ruler or Domain does not automatically raise the whole world. The change must alter the world's own stable structure.

World Rank growth may unlock new junctions, permit stronger inhabitants to remain unsuppressed, change ecology/resources, alter physical/metaphysical laws and attract higher-order attention.

## 5. World Rank decline

World Rank can also fall, temporarily or permanently.

Possible causes include:

- destruction of major law/energy anchors;
- dimensional wounds;
- collapse or corruption of multiple major Domains/Cores;
- loss of reality-supporting infrastructure;
- extraction/drain of foundational energy;
- catastrophic war;
- failed fusion;
- external predation/assimilation;
- fragmentation into weaker world pieces;
- deliberate sealing.

Decline is not automatically total apocalypse. A world may become weaker, unstable or partially cut off while remaining inhabited.

Severe decline can reduce what high-Rank entities can safely manifest, close junctions, mutate local laws, destroy resources and create sub-realms/scars.

Restoration is possible when the actual structural damage can be repaired.

## 6. Dimensional and world pressure

Personal Rank, World Rank and dimensional compatibility interact but are not the same scale.

An entity above a world's natural tolerance may enter when a valid route exists, but increasing mismatch can create:

- power/output suppression;
- reality resistance;
- higher costs;
- unstable manifestation;
- forced self-restraint;
- environmental damage;
- law conflicts;
- dimensional rejection/breach effects.

The higher the mismatch, the more severe the effect.

Specific vessels, avatars, seals, Domains, junction infrastructure, compatible bodies or authored abilities can mitigate this.

## 7. Junctions and traversal

Worlds/dimensions are connected by **Junctions**: stable, unstable or temporary relationships between reality nodes.

A Junction stores causal travel requirements rather than being only a menu unlock.

Requirements may depend on:

- relative World Rank;
- traveler Existence Rank;
- law compatibility;
- coordinates/knowledge;
- infrastructure;
- keys/artifacts;
- Domain authority;
- timing/astronomical state;
- dimensional events;
- cost/energy;
- story/world conditions.

Irregular events can create temporary breaches that bypass normal progression compatibility, preserving the existing rule that the world may occasionally expose content far above the protagonist's current safe range.

## 8. Dimensional preparation system

The gacha/contract preparation system is primarily a **metaphysical homeostatic defense mechanism** of Dimensions.

It does not require a speaking god or conscious administrator.

A Dimension facing long-horizon existential risk can create or expose mechanisms that:

- connect compatible Character Identities to Rulers;
- create Ruler-specific Manifestations;
- encourage accumulation and development of power;
- produce access to resources/opportunities;
- strengthen territorial/Domain structures;
- prepare distributed defenders rather than one chosen hero.

This explains why the protagonist is not guaranteed to be the sole chosen one and why NPC Rulers can use the same broad systems.

The exact historical origin of the mechanism, including whether some higher civilization/entity discovered, modified or weaponized it, remains story-level spoiler material.

## 9. Identity access architecture

Character acquisition does not pull a finite physical person out of another Ruler's world.

The dimensional system accesses a higher-order **Identity Pattern/Record** associated with a canonical Character Identity and instantiates a Ruler-specific Manifestation under the existing Character Identity -> Version -> Manifestation ontology.

The Pattern is not merely a database copy; it is a metaphysically valid existence-template linked to the canonical identity.

Different Rulers can therefore possess divergent Manifestations of one Character Identity without exhausting a unique global body.

Identity Pattern access may be limited by banner, compatibility, world state, dimensional reach, sealed content and other rules.

Whether Character Identities themselves can eventually perceive/understand the existence of multiple Manifestations is character/lore dependent.

## 10. Endgame threat ecology

True late-game threats are not one mandatory species/faction.

They form a broad ecology of outside-context existential threats, allowing radically different arcs and mechanics.

Threat origins may include both:

### Beyond-origin threats
Entities/phenomena from outside the ordinary dimensional structure or from higher regions not governed by familiar laws.

### Internal catastrophic threats
Former worlds/dimensions, civilizations, entities or systems that collapsed, transformed, corrupted or evolved into existential hazards from within the cosmology.

Potential threat families include, without limiting future content:

- dimensional predators that consume reality structures;
- assimilators that overwrite worlds into themselves;
- law-eroders that destroy or replace local rules;
- conceptual parasites;
- reality infestations/replicators;
- collapsed-dimension remnants;
- hostile self-propagating systems;
- high-order civilizations/entities;
- entities for which ordinary matter/space/time are only incidental constraints.

The taxonomy remains data-driven and expandable.

## 11. Threat interaction with normal progression

Endgame threats may possess their own:

- existence hierarchies;
- territory-like structures;
- reality fields;
- law/Authority equivalents;
- World-Fantasm-like personal realities;
- dimensional-scale weapons or infrastructure.

They are not automatically immune to ordinary systems. The same causal principles of Rank, Authority, counters, compatibility, knowledge and explicit bypasses continue to apply.

Likewise, their superiority is not simulated through hidden anti-player scaling. They are dangerous because their actual state/mechanics are dangerous.

## 12. Infinite expansion rule

The cosmology must never require the game to declare that the currently known highest structure is objectively the final layer of reality.

Higher structures may later be discovered if the new content:

- preserves previously established causal behavior;
- explains its relationship to lower structures;
- does not retroactively invalidate prior victories/progression without a real mechanism;
- uses versioned content definitions and stable IDs.

This permits HI3-like escalating metaphysical complexity while keeping the persistent save coherent.

## 13. Narrative protection

The architecture above is safe to expose as system design.

Specific answers about:

- the oldest known structures;
- the true origin of the preparation mechanism;
- the protagonist's origin;
- the exact highest threat;
- the historical cause of major collapses;
- hidden relationships between Dimensions/Identities;

remain spoiler-protected narrative content unless explicitly requested.
