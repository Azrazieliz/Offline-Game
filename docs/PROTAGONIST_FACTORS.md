# Protagonist Factors

This document records the current authoritative Factor architecture.

## 1. Causal ontology

A Factor is an actual incorporated aspect/piece of existence. It is never merely a race tag.

A Factor may change physiology, energy systems, instincts, senses, resistances, vulnerabilities, affinities, movement, transformations, resources, skills, Rank development and other mechanics.

Canonical player-facing concepts do not exist merely as decorative tags. A named Factor, Class, state or title must have causal, informational, systemic, social or historical consequences. Invisible implementation tags may exist for indexing/querying only; they cannot substitute for the causal model.

## 2. Identity, provenance and potency

- The same broad Factor may exist at radically different potency/quality.
- Provenance matters: source, acquisition event, lineage/subtype, initial quality, later integrations and evolutions are retained.
- Potency is not only one scalar; purity, maturity, completeness, special traits and source-specific capabilities may differ.
- Repeated materially equivalent acquisition normally integrates into the existing Factor instead of producing duplicate inert entries.

## 3. Growth

Factors can grow through use, training, Rank advancement, compatible absorption, maturation, transformation, environments, rituals, events and Factor-specific mechanics.

Growth may unlock anatomy, resource systems, forms, senses, resistances, vulnerabilities and techniques.

Factor growth may branch, mutate, refine, awaken or degrade where its own rules permit.

## 4. Canonical acquisition

There is no universal Factor token or generic acquisition recipe.

Each Factor defines its canonical acquisition route or routes. Examples include bloodline/inheritance, absorption, consumption, transplantation, infection, ritual, cultivation, exposure, transformation, implantation, symbiosis, experimentation, soul interaction and technology.

## 5. Freedom to attempt

The player is not blocked by arbitrary RPG permission checks from attempting a world-valid action merely because it is dangerous, inefficient, incompatible or likely to fail.

If the protagonist can physically/metaphysically attempt it, the simulation resolves the attempt and its consequences.

Failure, injury, mutation, corruption, resource loss, hostility or death replace arbitrary "you are not allowed" restrictions. Genuine impossibility and established world rules still remain causal.

## 6. Coexistence and dominance

- Acquired Factors remain causally present.
- A stronger Factor does not passively deactivate or erase a weaker one.
- Relative potency may dominate phenotype, output, transformation stability or conflict resolution without becoming a generic suppression status.
- Incompatibility exists only when those Factors' actual rules create it.
- Factor conflict may create instability, damage, mutation, altered forms or resource competition rather than deleting a Factor by fiat.

## 7. Synthesis, fusion and merge

Compatible Factors may synthesize/fuse/merge into a new composite Factor when the underlying rules justify a genuinely new existence state.

Comparable potency can make true fusion especially natural, but equal potency is not a universal numerical requirement.

Fusion is not automatically forced by compatibility. Parent provenance is retained, and whether parents remain independently expressible after fusion belongs to that specific fusion definition.

The engine must never accidentally count parent and composite as unrelated full-power bonuses.

## 8. Main / expressed Factors

There is no hard total Factor cap.

The protagonist may eventually possess hundreds of Factors while emphasizing one or more main/expressed Factors according to need.

Main/expressed status controls body-expression bias, development focus and UI/control prominence. It does not unequip, suppress or delete the rest.

As mastery improves, the player can deliberately shift emphasis among Factors. There is no arbitrary permanent Factor-slot limit.

## 9. Transformations

A transformation is a real bodily/existential state produced by Factor capabilities, not a cosmetic preset or generic Factor loadout.

A form may emphasize particular Factors more strongly while all owned Factors remain causally present.

Forms may be Factor-specific, composite, learned, evolved or event-created. Transition speed, duration, costs, morphology, vulnerabilities and retained abilities come from the real Factors involved.

Some forms may be freely reversible; others may require time, resources, conditions or a specific capability.

## 10. Skills, Classes and Rank

Factors may enable, mutate or rewrite skills because they change the body/existence performing those skills.

Factor-created techniques can contribute to automatic Class recognition; Class remains downstream of actual mastery.

A Factor may introduce an entirely new resource system, UI meter, movement model or combat grammar.

Factors may alter Rank-breakthrough requirements, but Factor potency does not map one-to-one to Rank.

## 11. UI / scale

The main character sheet surfaces important dominant/expressed Factors, resources/forms and recent changes.

A deeper Factor view exposes the full Factor graph, provenance, potency, development, synthesis ancestry, current expression and known interactions.

Filtering uses real relationships such as provenance, body system, lineage and function rather than arbitrary collectible tags.
