# Organic Ruler Progression

This document records the current authoritative Ruler-progression model. It supersedes older wording that implied every Ruler advances through one universal fixed rank ladder.

## 1. Continuous sovereignty state

Ruler progression is evaluated from actual state rather than a single ordered tier list.

Relevant dimensions include:

- personal / existence power;
- effective Authority;
- territory control, quality and scale;
- Domain/Core development;
- contracts and roster;
- subordinate Rulers;
- military and geopolitical reach;
- multi-world or dimensional reach;
- lore-specific structural achievements.

Different Rulers may therefore be powerful in fundamentally different ways. One may personally overpower almost anyone while controlling one small territory; another may rule a huge hierarchy through subordinates and infrastructure.

No title secretly grants catch-up power merely because it was recognized.

## 2. Milestone titles

- **Ruler** is the foundational sovereignty status.
- **Overlord** is a structural milestone reflecting actual superior authority over one or more subordinate Rulers. It is not a mandatory "next level" for every Ruler.
- Further titles are data-driven.
- A title may be universal when it describes a genuinely universal structure, or culture/world/faction/lore-specific where appropriate.
- Milestone recognition can be gained, lost or changed if the defining state changes.
- A title may persist as a historical honorific only when that title explicitly has a historical/earned form.

## 3. Presentation

The player-facing Ruler interface should emphasize concrete sovereignty facts and meaningful milestone titles rather than one misleading linear Rank bar.

Knowable milestone requirements may be shown directly. Hidden or lore-dependent milestones may be discovered through clues, institutions, appraisal or events.

Internal comparison scores may exist for simulation, AI, diagnostics or balancing but cannot replace the underlying state or serve as a universal verdict on who is stronger.
