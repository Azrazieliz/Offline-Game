# Organic Ruler Progression

This document records the current authoritative Ruler-progression model. It supersedes older wording that implied every Ruler advances through one universal fixed rank ladder.

## 1. Continuous sovereignty state

Ruler progression is evaluated from actual state rather than a single ordered tier list.

Relevant dimensions include:

- personal / existence power;
- effective Authority;
- territory control, quality and scale;
- Domain/Core development;
- contracts and roster;
- subordinate Rulers;
- military and geopolitical reach;
- multi-world or dimensional reach;
- lore-specific structural achievements.

Different Rulers may therefore be powerful in fundamentally different ways. One may personally overpower almost anyone while controlling one modest Territory; another may rule enormous space through hierarchy and infrastructure.

No title secretly grants catch-up power merely because it was recognized.

## 2. Universal sovereignty titles

There are only two universal sovereignty-status titles in the current architecture:

1. **Ruler**
2. **Overlord**

**Overlord is the highest universal sovereignty title.** There is no automatic Emperor -> God-King -> higher universal ladder above it.

Other political, cultural, religious, military or historical titles may exist without becoming universal Ruler-progression tiers.

## 3. Ruler

Ruler is the foundational sovereignty status.

A Ruler must actually possess/control Territory and have sufficient personal power / Authority / capability to establish and maintain that sovereignty.

A subordinate contract is not required. A solitary being can become a Ruler by holding Territory through sheer power or another valid mechanism.

## 4. Overlord

Overlord requires **both**:

- sufficient personal/existential power and effective Authority to support high-order sovereignty; and
- sufficiently great actual sovereignty.

Neither condition can substitute for the other.

A personally weak figurehead does not become a true Overlord merely because strong subordinates nominally answer to them. Likewise, an overwhelmingly powerful solitary combatant with only ordinary-scale sovereignty remains a Ruler until the sovereignty requirement is actually met.

### 4.1 Sovereignty requirement is structural, not a Domain counter

Overlord does **not** require an arbitrary minimum number of Domain Cores.

Valid Overlord-scale sovereignty can include:

- several independent Domains;
- a hierarchy of subordinate Rulers/Domains;
- direct and delegated holdings combined;
- world-spanning sovereignty;
- a single contiguous Territory whose actual reach/Authority spans most or all of a World;
- a single Territory or sovereignty structure that spans an entire Dimension;
- multi-world or cross-dimensional sovereignty;
- another equivalently high-order territorial structure.

The defining question is the actual scale and metaphysical/strategic weight of the sovereignty, not how many rows exist in a Territory table.

### 4.2 Overlord has no higher title, but it has magnitude

Overlord itself is not split into universal numbered sub-ranks.

However, the world can meaningfully distinguish radically different Overlords through actual state:

- personal Existence/Power Rank;
- effective Authority;
- territorial/world/dimensional scale;
- number and quality of Domains/Cores;
- subordinate Rulers;
- military/resource reach;
- dimensional infrastructure;
- historical peak;
- defensive and offensive capabilities.

UI/AI may summarize this as **Overlord Standing / Scope / Threat Estimate**, but that is descriptive analysis, not a new universal title ladder.

A high-end Overlord can therefore be incomparably stronger than another Overlord while both retain the same universal title.

## 5. Loss, defeat and historical state

Active titles follow current reality.

- If a Ruler loses the actual conditions for Ruler status after applicable reclamation/grace rules resolve, active Ruler status is lost.
- If an Overlord loses enough personal capability or sovereignty that Overlord conditions are no longer true, active Overlord status is lost.
- Losing several Domains does **not** automatically remove Overlord if the remaining sovereignty is still Overlord-scale; one dimension-spanning Territory may be sufficient.
- Defeat does not erase personal strength, prior mastery or historical peak sovereignty.

The Chronicle/state model therefore separately stores:

- current sovereignty title;
- historical peak title;
- known former titles;
- peak territorial/world/dimensional scope;
- current vs peak personal/Authority state.

This permits situations where a former high-end Overlord has been stripped of most sovereignty and is publicly mistaken for weak even though their personal power remains terrifying. The reverse can also occur: a politically vast Ruler may lose the personal capability required for Overlord and cease to qualify despite retaining much infrastructure.

Reacquiring valid conditions can restore active Overlord status; it is not a once-per-life achievement lock.

## 6. Joint sovereignty

Joint/co-sovereign structures are valid when the actual Authority and territorial rules support them.

Several beings may jointly hold the same high-order sovereignty and may all be active Overlords if **each** independently satisfies:

- the personal/Authority threshold; and
- genuine co-sovereign authority over the Overlord-scale structure.

A subordinate, regent, administrator or symbolic co-monarch does not become an Overlord merely by association.

Joint Overlords may divide functions, Authority channels, Domains, military command or metaphysical responsibilities according to the actual structure.

## 7. Presentation and knowledge

The Ruler UI emphasizes concrete sovereignty facts and capabilities rather than a misleading linear Ruler-rank bar.

Knowable milestone requirements may be shown directly. Hidden/lore-dependent aspects may instead be learned through clues, institutions, appraisal, intelligence or events.

Current title, historical title and estimated Overlord standing are knowledge-dependent for unknown foreign entities. A defeated former Overlord can therefore be misidentified or underestimated if the observer lacks history/intelligence.

Internal comparison scores may exist for simulation, AI, diagnostics or balancing but cannot replace the underlying state or serve as a universal verdict on who is stronger.
