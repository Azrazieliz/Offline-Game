# Character Progression and Grand Convergence

This document records the current character-development architecture.

## 1. Current Rarity

- Origin Rarity is immutable.
- Current Rarity reflects how far a particular Manifestation has reinforced/developed the collectible character framework.
- Current Rarity partly reflects development but is not a direct combat-power measurement.
- Rank, level, skills, equipment, route synergy, counters and encounter relevance can make a lower-Current-Rarity Manifestation outperform a higher-Current-Rarity one.
- Current Rarity is normally monotonic. Ordinary weakening/regression is expressed through Rank/effective state, injuries, seals or route mechanics instead of routine Rarity loss.

## 2. Rarity promotion

- Promotion uses one common technical framework.
- Requirements are character-/Manifestation-specific.
- Requirements may include materials, feats, story/world conditions, development-route states, transformations, victories, breakthroughs and other canonical prerequisites.
- Two copies of the same Identity may reach the same Current Rarity through different histories.

## 3. Open-ended development routes

- Evolution, Awakening and Corruption are initial route families, not a closed taxonomy.
- New route families can be added data-first without changing the core ontology.
- Routes may branch, recurse, combine, mutate and interact.
- Compatible development states can coexist.
- Individual states may remain mutually exclusive when their actual fiction/body/state makes simultaneous expression impossible.
- Corruption is morally neutral as a system. It is one possible route, comparable to an Alter-style transformation, and is not automatically evil, worse or a debuff.

## 4. Multiple Manifestations of one Identity

- The same Ruler may own multiple persistent Manifestations of one Character Identity.
- Each copy has its own stable ID and independent Rank/Level, Current Rarity, equipment, learned skills, route graph, forms, history and reinforcement state.
- Duplicate pulls normally create new Manifestation copies.
- Local combat identity exclusivity remains the default unless a mechanic explicitly permits simultaneous copies.
- Copies can otherwise be trained, equipped and assigned independently.

## 5. Skill accumulation and kit integration

- Learned techniques can accumulate far beyond the visible active-kit size.
- The compact kit evolves through character synthesis plus player-directed build choices.
- Minor compatible techniques may be integrated into passives, attack properties or evolved skills.
- Major route commitments and build-defining integrations remain player-directed.
- Different copies can therefore express radically different compact kits.

## 6. Maximum reinforcement

Max reinforcement is Manifestation-specific and may require:
- the current intended Rarity/Rank ceiling;
- route-specific progression;
- character-specific breakthrough conditions;
- relevant skill/equipment development;
- other authored requirements.

Because development routes are intentionally open-ended, completion cannot mean exhausting every theoretical future branch. Each Character Identity instead defines convergence-relevant major lineages/achievements.

## 7. Grand Convergence

Working name: **Grand Convergence**.

- It is the identity-level finalization for multiple divergent copies of the same Character Identity.
- A character-specific Convergence Set requires multiple fully reinforced Manifestations representing required distinct major development lineages.
- Count and required lineages are content data, not one universal number.
- Selected source Manifestations fuse into one persistent **Grand Manifestation**.
- Source progression histories, forms, route provenance and achievements remain recorded inside the Grand Manifestation.
- Grand Convergence is explicit and consequential.

## 8. Grand Manifestation

- Inherits access to completed route knowledge/forms from all source Manifestations.
- Compatible route benefits may be synthesized.
- Mutually exclusive states do not blindly stack; they remain selectable/switchable unless an authored synthesis resolves the incompatibility.
- Power is not produced by naively adding all source stats together.
- Future max-reinforced copies/new lineages may later be integrated into the existing Grand Manifestation.
- The result remains one canonical Character Identity and still obeys that Identity's World Fantasm, Transcendence and narrative rules unless specific Grand content evolves them.

## 9. Content contract

Character content defines stable Identity, Origin Rarity, base assets/Versions, rarity-promotion requirements, route families/nodes, compatibility/exclusivity, forms, kit-integration rules, max-reinforcement criteria, Convergence Set requirements and Grand synthesis rules.

Validation must reject circular prerequisites, impossible convergence requirements, silent history loss and duplicate-stacking exploits.
