# Design Collaboration Operating Rule

This document records how Creative Director decisions and delegated system design should be handled from this checkpoint onward.

- Ask the Creative Director only **large subsystem-level questions** that materially affect player experience, ontology, progression architecture, or narrative direction.
- Do not default to long micro-questionnaires.
- Intermediate rules, edge cases, UI details, implementation-compatible behavior and ordinary design glue are delegated to the assistant.
- Delegated design must remain compatible with the cumulative authoritative baseline and the Creative Director's newest explicit direction.
- After each design pass, report the resulting design compactly: what was decided, why it fits the existing rules, and which genuinely major choices remain.
- The Creative Director may override any delegated decision. The newest explicit direction supersedes it and must be propagated through design and implementation documentation.
- Plot twists, hidden identities and future narrative answers remain spoiler-protected unless the Creative Director explicitly asks to inspect them.
